builds, for the App Store's Steam artwork (`../make-art.py` makes
`../art/`). The logos and names belong to their projects; they are shown
only to identify each app. Where no license is stated for the icon itself,

The artwork places each logo, scaled (RetroArch's recolored white, chiaki-ng's
corners rounded), on a background made from its colors, with the app's name
and summary. The artwork made from a logo is under that logo's license;
Prism Launcher's (CC BY-SA 4.0) is shared under CC BY-SA 4.0.

Not shipped, so their artwork shows the name only:

- DuckStation: its README credits the icon to icons8, under icons8's
  terms, not DuckStation's license.
- BigPEmu: closed freeware that may not be redistributed without its terms
  document; its icon is 128 px.
